Dynamic Card Mapping™

RPL Educator Companion incorporates an original evidence-mapping implementation identified as Dynamic Card Mapping™.

Dynamic Card Mapping™ provides a visual workflow for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) assessment in which individual pieces of applicant evidence are represented as digital cards.

Evidence cards can be interactively mapped and stacked against the requirements of a unit of competency, including:

* Performance Criteria
* Knowledge Evidence
* Performance Evidence
* Assessment Conditions
* other applicable competency requirements

The application uses the resulting evidence mapping to assist with identification of potential evidence gaps and supports structured supplementary questioning and assessment to address those gaps.

Dynamic Card Mapping™ was conceived and developed by Stuart Anderson / Fastigiata Lab in 2026.
